# operations/alerts.md

Purpose: Alert routing pages the on-call engineer only after three consecutive failed health probes; one successful probe resets the failure count, while missing probes remain unknown.

## Themes
- on-call alerting
- health-probe failure thresholds
- consecutive-failure state
- success-based reset
- unknown telemetry handling

## Citations
- `operations/alerts.md:1-3` — Defines the paging threshold, reset rule, and missing-probe semantics.

## Gotchas
- Do not page for fewer than three consecutive failures.
- A single success clears the entire consecutive-failure count.
- Missing probes are unknown: they are not successful and must not reset the count.

## Recipes
- Implement a counter that increments on failure, pages at three consecutive failures, and resets to zero on success.
- Handle missing probes as an explicit unknown state; leave the failure counter unchanged.
