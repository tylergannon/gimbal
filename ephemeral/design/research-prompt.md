# Research prompt (150 words)

Research interface concepts for Gimbal, a tool for observing and interacting with agent workflows written in Go. Find concrete references across agent orchestration products and adjacent domains: workflow engines, tracing tools, debuggers, incident consoles, and creative canvases. We need run discovery, historical review, live steering and interviews, plus an interactive workflow map with zoom and drilldown. Seek varied interaction models, not variations of one dashboard. For each strong example, provide an authoritative source, a screenshot or precisely located video frame, what is visibly demonstrated, and what Gimbal could borrow. Distinguish observed behavior from inference. Explain tradeoffs for concurrency, repeated calls, long histories, attention requests, and preserving context while navigating. Note access restrictions and do not fabricate screenshots. Recommend three distinct design directions, with examples supporting each. Keep product features separate from visual inspiration; do not expand Gimbal’s scope. Prefer a few deeply examined references over an uncurated gallery of links.
