# Tern Archive access
Visitor rules effective 2026-04-01. The Reading Hall admits visitors aged 16 or older. The Exhibition Wing admits all ages. This age distinction is about the buildings, not competing versions of one rule.

Reading Hall entry requires a free researcher card. Exhibition Wing entry does not require a researcher card. A card identifies the researcher; it does not reserve a seat or request a box of material. A person visiting both buildings must follow each building's access rules.

These rules do not describe ticket prices for special evening events. No residency requirement is stated. A guide should not invent one from the word researcher. The rules apply from the effective date and do not establish what the archive did in earlier years.
