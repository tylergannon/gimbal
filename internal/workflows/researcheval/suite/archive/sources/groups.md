# Group visit bookings
A group booking may include at most 14 visitors. Each booked tour lasts 50 minutes. The group limit is a number of people; the duration is not an arrival window. A larger party must request separate bookings rather than assume it can enter as one group.

Groups must book at least 6 calendar days before arrival. The rule uses calendar days, including weekends, rather than working days. Booking a tour does not by itself request manuscript boxes or grant Reading Hall access.

A tour covers the Exhibition Wing. Researchers accompanying a tour still need the appropriate card for the Reading Hall. The document does not state a price for a group booking or a discount for school groups.
