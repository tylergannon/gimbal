# Requesting stored material
Material requested before 11:00 is normally delivered by 15:00 the same day. Requests placed at or after 11:00 are normally delivered the next opening day. These are expected delivery times, not guarantees that the item is unrestricted or available.

A researcher may hold at most 4 boxes at a desk simultaneously. A fifth box can be requested after one is returned. This desk limit is not a daily request limit. The document gives no upper bound on the total number of boxes requested over an entire day.

The April process replaces a prior arrangement that delivered all requests on the next opening day. A historical description of that arrangement does not contradict this effective process.
