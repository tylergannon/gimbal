# Self-service digitization
One scan job may include at most 30 pages. The maximum output size of a scan job is 90 MiB. A scan job is stopped at whichever cap is reached first. The output-size cap is not a per-page limit.

Personal photography without flash is allowed in the Reading Hall unless the item's restriction card prohibits it. Permission to photograph is not permission to publish; these documents give no publication license.

Staff-assisted scans follow a separate process and no page cap is stated here for that process. Do not extend the self-service figure to a service this reference does not cover. Images should be checked before deleting the local temporary copy.
