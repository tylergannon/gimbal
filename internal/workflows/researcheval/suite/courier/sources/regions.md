# Courier regional bulletin
Published: 2026-03-03. As of this bulletin, v2 is available in North and West. South remains on v1. Statements in older launch material that only North supports v2 are historical, not the current availability statement.

Accounts select a region when their queue is created. The bulletin does not document cross-region replication or residency guarantees. Availability of a region is not evidence that every feature is enabled there. No date for v2 in South has been announced here.

The North launch was first announced on 2026-01-15. The later West announcement expands that list rather than negating the original historical launch event. Use the publication date when comparing these statements.
